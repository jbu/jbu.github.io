Like ~everyone, I've been working out how to best use LLMs in my [day job](@/cv.md), but given the
maelstrom of hype, hope and hysteria it'll be useful to keep some sort of diary about it. Mostly to
keep my head straight, but given how raw everything is I think as a community we should work more
in the open and collaborate, so I'll air it all here in public. I'll put things in reverse chronology.

## Previously...


The best time to start this would have been a couple of years ago. The second best time is now, so now it is.
Here's a catch-up from befuddled memory.


### Earlier

Early systems were pretty underwhelming. At the time (2024?) I was mostly working in databricks, and they
started jamming chat bots, autocomplete, and everything else into every nook. Mostly they produced garbage and
I just deleted the suggestions (which I usually hadn't asked for) - It was all very "Cirius Cybernetics Corporation",
and Databricks weren't unusual - it was everywhere. Mostly it slowed me sligtly as I removed any slop, but I usually left it enabled to track progress.
